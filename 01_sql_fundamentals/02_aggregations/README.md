- SUM()

- HAVING

- Sorting aggregated results

## 🎯 Objective

Practice using SQL aggregate functions to transform employee-level data into meaningful business metrics.

## 📊 Business Questions

The analysis answers questions such as:

1. How many employees are in the company?
2. What is the total salary expense?
3. What is the average employee salary?
4. What are the minimum and maximum salaries?
5. How many employees work in each department?
6. What is the total salary expense by department?
7. What is the average salary by department?
8. Which departments have salary expenses above ₹100,000?
9. Which departments have at least 3 employees and an average salary above ₹50,000?
10. Which cities have at least 2 employees and total salary expenses above ₹90,000?
